# References — for the Space Apps project page ("References" field)

Everything the Marswalk Planner uses, by publisher. Copy the list into the project form.
Full provenance is in `config.py`, `weather_sources.csv`, `radiation_data/README.md`,
`frontend/route_models/README.md` and `gear/sources.py`.

## Elevation and surface (built into each area's data cube)

- NASA/ESA/USGS — Mars MGS MOLA–MEX HRSC Blended DEM Global 200 m v2 (USGS Astrogeology):
  https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/Mars/HRSC_MOLA_Blend/Mars_HRSC_MOLA_BlendDEM_Global_200mp_v2.tif
- USGS / NASA JPL — Mars 2020 Terrain Relative Navigation products for Jezero: CTX DEM 20 m, CTX orthomosaic 6 m,
  HiRISE DTM 1 m: https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/mars2020_trn/
- Esri / NASA — MOLA/HRSC 200 m 3D terrain service (MDEM200M), "OnMars": https://astro.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/OnMars
- NASA Mars Odyssey THEMIS day and night IR mosaics (USGS Astrogeology WMS): https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/cgi-bin/mapserv?map=/maps/mars/mars_simp_cyl.map
- THEMIS-derived thermal inertia, global quadrangles (Huang 2026, Zenodo, CC BY 4.0): https://zenodo.org/records/23527756
- THEMIS-derived thermal inertia of Jezero crater (Zenodo): https://zenodo.org/records/4931348
- NASA MGS TES bolometric albedo, global 7.4 km (USGS Astrogeology): https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/Mars_MGS_TES_Albedo_mosaic_global_7410m.tif
- NASA MGS TES thermal inertia and special products (PDS Geosciences Node): https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mgs/tesspecial.html
- NASA MRO CRISM mineral products via the PDS Orbital Data Explorer: https://oderest.rsl.wustl.edu/live2/ —
  summary parameters after Viviano-Beck et al. (2014), JGR Planets 119, 1403
- Jezero CRISM dominant-mineral map (Saranathan & Parente, Zenodo): https://zenodo.org/records/5575824

## Atmosphere, weather and radiation

- OpenMARS reanalysis (Holmes, Lewis & Patel), CC BY 4.0: https://doi.org/10.21954/ou.rd.c.4278950 —
  retrieved from the ARCO-OpenMARS conversion (Bhattacharya): https://doi.org/10.57967/hf/8741
- LMD dust scenarios and Montabone et al. dust climatology (CC BY-SA 3.0): https://www-mars.lmd.jussieu.fr/mars/dust_scenarios/
- Surface effective dose model, Zhang & Guo (2022), AtRIS/GEANT4, CC BY 4.0: https://zenodo.org/records/5953666 —
  study: https://doi.org/10.1029/2021JE007157
- NASA Curiosity RAD measurements (context at Gale): https://science.nasa.gov/resource/radiation-measurements-on-mars/ ;
  PDS RAD data: https://doi.org/10.17189/1519761

  https://pds-atmospheres.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/PERSEVERANCE/meda.html

## Mission, suit and vehicle numbers

- NASA SBIR topic EVA.2.S26B (Mars suit oxygen sizing) and NASA EVA baseline figures — suit oxygen use
- Minetti et al. (2002) — energy cost of walking on slopes
- Chappell, Hoffman & Bekdash (2022), "Human Mars Mission Surface Power Impacts on Timeline and Traverse
  Capabilities", NTRS 20210022250 — pressurized rover battery, loads and traverse range
- NASA Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle figures — open crew rover battery and range
- NASA/JPL MSL MMRTG power figures; NASA/JPL MER solar power figures; ESA Rosalind Franklin power figures
- Mars Science Laboratory Engineering User Guide — slope vs. wheel slip thresholds:
  https://marsoweb.nas.nasa.gov/landingsites/msl/memoranda/MSL_Eng_User_Guide_v3.pdf
- Equipment masses and power: `MISSION_EQUIPMENT_RESEARCH.md` and `gear/sources.py`
- COSPAR Special Regions and NASA Human Exploration Zones (landing and protection rules, `config.py`)
