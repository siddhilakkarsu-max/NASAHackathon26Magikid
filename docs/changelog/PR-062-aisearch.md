# PR #62: Plan where the camera looks, loading progress bar, 13× faster mineral maps, zoom to the route

**What changed**
- **No Jezero default.**
  - The planner starts with no area loaded.
  - Plans, the assistant and the area maps (slope, minerals and so on) use the place in the middle of the map,
    at any zoom. Before, zoomed out, they kept using Jezero.
  - Choosing an area map loads the spot under the camera instead of flying to Jezero.
  - Picking a named region still flies there.
- **Progress bar while an area loads.**
  - A bar at the top of the map shows real progress, e.g. "Loading Mars around 12.20° N, 25.30° E · map layer
    2 of 5 · 2 s", then a smaller bar for the mineral observations that load in the background.
  - The builder writes `STAGE layers N`, and `_build_progress` in `app.py` turns its per-layer log lines into
    `terrain_progress` / `minerals_progress`.
- **Mineral maps about 13× faster** (`build_cube.py`).
  - GDAL read the band-sequential CRISM images in about 1,000 16 KB HTTP requests per product, taking about
    125 s each from PDS.
  - The rows over the area are now one byte range per band, fetched in parallel (at most 12 at once across a
    build) and warped locally. That takes about 8 s, with the same values: max difference 2e-10.
  - Other file layouts still use GDAL.
  - `tests/test_crism_fast.py`.
- **Zoom to the route when a plan finishes.**
  - The camera always flies to the selected route.
  - Loading the area for a plan no longer counts as a map-layer change, so the goal overlay shows again. The
    manual plan's walk-back overlay had the same problem.

**Why**
- **Jezero default:** planning with the map zoomed out silently used Jezero.
- **Progress:** loading showed only a timer.
- **Mineral maps:** they took minutes.
- **Zoom:** the camera stayed where it was after planning.

**How to try it**
- Start the planner, look at any place on Mars, then plan a mission or turn on Slope. The bar shows the area
  loading, and the camera flies to the route when the plan is ready.
- A new area is fully ready, minerals included, in about 15–20 s.

**Merge conflicts**
- One conflict in `frontend/index.html`, at the startup fly-to line. #60 had changed it to
  `if (initialLayer && !globalLayerSelected()) flyTo(current.rect);`, and this branch had removed it.
- Kept #60's line, at the author's choice. With no area loaded at startup the line doesn't run, but it keeps
  #60's intent if an area is ever loaded there again.
