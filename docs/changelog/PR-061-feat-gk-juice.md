## Summary

A "game feel" layer for the planner (`frontend/juice.js`, `frontend/juice.css`, one deferred `<script>` line in
`index.html`). It only adds feedback on top of the planner's own controls and never changes what they do.

- **Feel:** buttons, chips, section headers and menu items squish and spring back with a ripple and a soft
  synthesized sound. Chips sparkle on, the rail's active item is a gliding pill, sections rise in. Sliders fill,
  tick and show a value bubble (🌙 22:00, 🌪️ Ls 250°, 🎒 4 h EVA). Map clicks send a sonar ping.
- **Planning:** Plan mission breathes when there is a goal and shows stripes while planning. Routes found fire
  confetti cannons. Route cards slide in, count their scores up and tilt under the pointer.
- **Explorer XP:** actions earn XP with combos, 15 badges (2 secret) unlock with a toast, and there are named levels with a
  level-up burst. A ring in the rail opens the badge collection, a sound toggle and "Reset progress".

**Try it:** open the planner, click Start exploring (Liftoff badge), switch on the Radiation chip, drag the time
slider to night, click the level ring in the left rail.

Progress is kept in `localStorage` (`jz.v1`). Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. No merge conflicts.
