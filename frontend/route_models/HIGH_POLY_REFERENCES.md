# High-poly reference audit

The route GLBs are compact, procedural, animation-ready visualizations. They are not silently presented as NASA flight-CAD clones. The following primary sources were used for the silhouette and hardware audit:

| Asset | Primary source | What was verified |
|---|---|---|
| Perseverance | [NASA rover components](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/rover-components/) and [NASA Perseverance 3D model](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-2020-perseverance-rover-2/) | six wheels, rocker-bogie suspension, mast/cameras, deck and hardware modules |
| Curiosity-class | [NASA Curiosity 3D model](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/curiosity-rover-3d-model/) | six-wheel MSL silhouette, mast, deck and arm proportions |
| Spirit/Opportunity (MER) | [NASA Spirit mission page](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mer-spirit/) and [NASA rover comparison image](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/rovers-and-sojourner/) | MER mast, dish, instrument arm and rocker-bogie cues |
| Sojourner | [NASA rover comparison image](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/rovers-and-sojourner/) | compact three-wheel-per-side Pathfinder/Sojourner layout and solar canopy |
| Space Exploration Vehicle concepts | [NASA SEV 3D resource](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/space-exploration-vehicle/) | pressurized cabin, airlock, roof equipment and six-wheel concept cues |
| Lunar open rover cues | [NASA Apollo 17 LRV resource](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/apollo-17-gene-cernan-in-lunar-roving-vehicle/) | open frame, seats, rack bars and exposed wheel layout |
| Rosalind Franklin | [ESA ExoMars rover project](https://explorationscience.esa.int/project/exomars-rosalind-franklin-rfm/) | solar wings, mast and drill-housing cues |
| Zhurong | [CNSA Zhurong mission release](https://www.cnsa.gov.cn/english/n6465652/n6465653/c6840321/content.html) | copper-toned body, solar petals, mast and high-gain dish cues |

The official NASA downloadable models are retained as reference targets in the local rover-model reference pack. The release bundle contains the procedural route GLBs only so the app keeps its looping animations and small streaming footprint.
